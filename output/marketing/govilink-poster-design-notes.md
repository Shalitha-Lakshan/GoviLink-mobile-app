# GoviLink marketing poster

Created with the built-in image generation tool.

## Handoff notes

- Audience: Sri Lankan farmers, produce buyers, and delivery partners.
- Features: produce listings, custom buyer requests, order and delivery status updates.
- Download/demo link and support contact are intentional placeholders, approved by the user. Replace them once confirmed; add a scannable QR code only after its destination is available.
- The phone screen is an illustrative mockup, not a captured app screenshot.
- Portrait design inspired by A4. Check the image resolution before selecting a physical print size.

## Generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.
Create a polished, high-resolution portrait marketing poster for GoviLink, a Sri Lankan farm-to-buyer mobile app, for a university User Experience Engineering assignment. A4 portrait proportions, approximately 2480 x 3508 if supported. Full flat poster artwork, no external frame or wall mockup. Crisp perfectly legible English typography and strong hierarchy, ample margins.
Design: premium contemporary agricultural brand, deep navy #0B2545, forest green #006837, leaf green #16A34A, warm ivory background. Restrained editorial composition with a rich green organic sweeping curve and subtle topographic contour lines. Photoreal fresh carrots, tomatoes, leafy greens in a wooden produce crate at the lower edge of the central hero, a soft distant Sri Lankan hillside growing field. Sophisticated and uncluttered, not a generic dense infographic.
Top: small elegant G leaf emblem (navy-to-green G with two leaves inside) beside exact wordmark "GoviLink". Small eyebrow "GROWN LOCALLY. CONNECTED DIGITALLY."
Large headline, exact text:
"Fresh harvest.
Stronger connections."
Subheading exact: "Connecting Sri Lankan farmers, buyers and delivery partners."
Central hero: one large realistic modern smartphone, slightly angled but its screen readable, paired with the produce crate. Phone screen is a representative illustrative marketplace mockup, navy header "GoviLink", title "Fresh Harvest Marketplace", white/very light gray background, green accents, search field "Search fresh produce", three clean produce photo cards "Fresh Carrots", "Tomatoes", "Leafy Greens", green "Order Produce" buttons, and bottom tabs "Market", "Requests", "Orders". No fabricated reviews, user counts, prices, certification or GPS map. Small readable caption directly beneath phone: "Illustrative app preview".
Below hero: three balanced features with small coherent outline icons and exact headings and descriptions:
"List your harvest" / "Share available produce with buyers."
"Find what you need" / "Browse crops or post a custom request."

Small inset white outlined placeholder rectangle with link icon, NO QR pattern, exact label "DOWNLOAD / DEMO LINK" and exact placeholder "[Add your app link]"

Preserve a visually strong, professional marketing poster with clearly legible real text.
